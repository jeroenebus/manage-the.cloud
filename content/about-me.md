Welcome to my blog! I'm Jeroen Ebus, a modern workspace and cloud consultant at [PeopleRock](https://peoplerock.nl).

I've worked in IT since 2007, for various employers and customers, and my focus has always been end user computing. Today that mostly means Microsoft Intune, Windows, Entra and Azure.

On this blog I share what I learn on that journey: step-by-step guides with plenty of screenshots, so you can follow along in your own tenant.
